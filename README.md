<h1>🧠 Excuse Generator</h1>
A fun and simple Excuse Generator that creates random excuses at the click of a button!
Perfect for when you’re late to class, miss a deadline, or just need a creative reason to get out of trouble 😅.

<h2>🚀 Features</h2>

-Generates random, funny excuses.

-Easy-to-use web interface.

-Lightweight and fast.


<h2>🛠️ Tech Stack</h2>

-Language: JavaScript

-Styling: TailwindCSS / CSS3
